## Choosing a document

Use `product.md` for confirmed product intent, and `architecture.md` for the system as actually implemented. Before a substantial change, write a design in `designs/YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`; retain designs ready for review, accepted, or kept as history. Design statuses are `proposed`, `accepted`, `in-progress`, `implemented`, and `rejected`. Update the design as the work progresses and update `architecture.md` when implementation changes the system.

For a lasting technical choice with meaningful alternatives, write one ADR in `decisions/NNNN-short-title.md`, numbering from `0001` in sequence. ADR statuses are `proposed`, `accepted`, `rejected`, and `superseded`. Its title must name the chosen approach, not merely the subject. A new ADR supersedes an accepted decision; mark the old one `superseded`, link to its replacement, and preserve its original rationale. Templates are exempt from the dated/numbered filename convention. Do not create an ADR just to fill the directory.

`docs/tmp/` is a local, Git-ignored workspace for drafts, plans, collected context, and task notes. Its contents are not current decisions. When a draft is ready for review, move the relevant content to `designs/` or `decisions/` as `proposed`. At task end, remove obsolete temporary material and promote lasting findings to permanent documents. The empty directory is intentionally not tracked.

## Required review

Each new design or ADR starts as `proposed` and goes to an independent architecture reviewer: a separate reviewer agent or a person who did not write it, given the context, alternatives, and related documents. Address material findings; repeat review if the choice materially changes. Record only an actual review outcome in the document. Once review is complete and no material issues remain, the author may set `accepted` without routine human approval. If no independent reviewer is available, finish the other work, keep the document `proposed`, and report the missing review. Proposed designs and ADRs do not describe implemented architecture.
